## คำอธิบายยาว (About This Game)
**What you draw is what fights.**
Sketch a sword and it slashes. Draw a hammer and it shakes the ground. Every weapon gets its own signature skill built from what you made.

- **8 ready-made heroes** — from the flame swordsman Blaze to Inkling, a hero who walked out of a sketchbook
- **Import your own** — bring .glb characters from Blender and skill packs (.dfskill) made by other players
- **37+ skills** — meteors, thunder, time stop, dimension cuts, each with its own look
- **14 elements** that counter each other
- **Skill-card gacha with no real money** — earn gold by fighting, pity guaranteed every 30 pulls
- **Wave battles and bosses** — telegraphed attacks you can read and dodge
- **Auto mode** — let your hero fight on its own and watch your build work
