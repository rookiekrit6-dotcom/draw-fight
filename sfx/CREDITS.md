# Sound credits

Every recorded sound in `bank.json` is public domain (CC0 1.0). No credit is required. These creators are listed anyway, with thanks.

- Kenney (kenney.nl): Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and Sci-Fi Sounds packs.
- rubberduck (OpenGameArt): 100 CC0 SFX, 100 CC0 SFX #2, 75 CC0 breaking/falling/hit SFX, 25 CC0 bang/firework SFX, 40 CC0 water/splash/slime SFX, and 80 CC0 creature SFX.
- artisticdude (OpenGameArt): RPG Sound Pack and Swishes Sound Pack.
- Ogrebane (OpenGameArt): Battle Sound Effects.

The files were taken from public GitHub mirrors that list the original sources: Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index and seyedpouramir/alvana-audio.

All files were trimmed, made mono and set to the same loudness. Some were pitched or filtered.

The looping fire sound was assembled for this game from a recorded thruster roar plus short snaps cut from recorded rock and wood impacts.
